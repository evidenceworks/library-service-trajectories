# Third-party material

Finnish Public Libraries Statistics supplies the reported library hours, worked FTE, population fields and source labels for 2017 and 2022–2025. The database states that its statistical data use CC BY 4.0. Required attribution: Finnish Public Libraries Statistics, relevant year(s), Ministry of Education and Culture, Finland, https://tilastot.kirjastot.fi/. The data here are extracted, combined into consistent territories and annotated; they are not the original workbooks. [Provider license statement](https://tilastot.kirjastot.fi/intro.php?lang=en), checked October 6, 2026.

Statistics Finland supplies the prior official population references and annual municipal classifications. Its open statistical data and public service content use CC BY 4.0 unless separately stated. Attribution: Source: Statistics Finland, population structure and municipal classifications. The repository contains the derived analytic references and identifiers; it does not redistribute confidential microdata or paid database material. [Terms of use](https://stat.fi/en/about-us/get-to-know-statistics-finland/legislation/terms-of-use) and [open data and classifications](https://stat.fi/en/services/statistical-data-services/open-data-and-interfaces), checked October 6, 2026.

Provider attribution remains required when these data are reused. The repository license does not claim ownership of provider content. Linked instructions and other documents remain subject to their own rightsholder terms; no general license is asserted for them. NumPy and Matplotlib are installed separately and retain their upstream licenses. No dependency source, font file or third-party document is bundled.
